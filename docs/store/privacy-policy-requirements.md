# Privacy policy: requirements and draft

Status: **no privacy policy exists in the repo and none is linked from the app.** Google Play requires a public, non-editable, non-geofenced URL (a normal web page, not a PDF) for every app. The URL is entered in Play Console (App content -> Privacy policy) and should also be reachable from inside the app. The app currently has no link to one (About screen shows only name and version, src/ui/About.ts). Adding an in-app link would be a JS change (OTA-able) if it opens via the existing `Linking.openURL` path in App.tsx / src/shell/navigation.ts; note that `https:` links currently load inside the game WebView (`IN_WEBVIEW` regex), so a link would replace the game page unless navigation.ts is changed. Recommend a tiny change first.

## A. Facts the policy must state for this app (all verified from code unless marked)

1. **Who**: Hot Attic Games (developer name from About screen; OWNER TO CONFIRM legal name/entity and country).
2. **Contact**: support email. Code uses `hotatticgames@gmail.com` (src/util/bugReport.ts). OWNER TO CONFIRM it is the address to publish.
3. **No account, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no social features.**
4. **Data stored on the device only**: rider profile name (typed or randomly generated, up to 32 chars), snowflake balance, upgrade levels, records and lifetime stats, milestones and daily-challenge progress, settings (music volume, sound-effect volume, vibration), and any unbanked in-progress run. Stored in IndexedDB inside the app's WebView storage. A short rotating error log (up to 60 entries) is stored in the WebView's localStorage. None of this is uploaded.
5. **Update checks (network)**: the app contacts Expo's update service (`u.expo.dev`) shortly after launch, about every 90 seconds while it is open, and when the player taps "Check for updates", to download game updates. Expo receives: IP address and standard HTTP metadata; platform (android); app runtime version and update channel; IDs of the currently running and embedded updates; a random per-install identifier (`EAS-Client-ID`) generated by the update library; and, only after a native fatal error, up to 1024 characters of that error log. Transport is HTTPS. No gameplay data, profile names or personal data are sent. Expo's own handling is governed by Expo's privacy policy (OWNER TO CONFIRM link and to review Expo's terms/DPA).
6. **Bug report / feature request**: tapping these opens the player's own email app with a pre-filled draft addressed to the support email. The draft contains whatever the player writes plus a diagnostic block: app version, Android build number, runtime/channel/update IDs and source commit, the WebView user-agent string (includes Android version and device model), and the last 30 log lines of the previous and current sessions (error messages with timestamps). The player can edit or delete any of it. Nothing is sent unless the player presses send in their email app. The developer will use received emails only to investigate the problem and reply.
7. **Permissions** (from permissions-inventory.md): Internet and network state (update checks), vibration (optional, can be switched off in Settings). No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or storage access is needed. (Do NOT publish this sentence until the final merged manifest is checked.)
8. **Audio**: music and sound effects play locally. The app does not record audio.
9. **Retention and deletion**: local data stays until the player deletes the profile (Profile picker -> edit -> Delete profile), clears app storage, or uninstalls. Emails received are kept as long as needed to handle the report; they are deleted on request. Expo's update logs are held by Expo under its own retention (OWNER TO CONFIRM).
10. **Children**: the app is not designed for / directed to children under 13 (or state otherwise). OWNER TO DECIDE; this must match the Play target-audience declaration.
11. **Backups**: if Android Auto Backup is enabled for the app (not verified, see data-safety.md section 5), the OS may back up app data to the player's Google account under Google's terms. State whichever applies.
12. **No sale or sharing** of personal data; no advertising; no tracking across apps.
13. **Changes to the policy** and effective date.
14. **Your rights**: state those that apply in the owner's jurisdiction (OWNER TO CONFIRM: GDPR/UK GDPR if EU/UK users or owner; CCPA/CPRA; others). Since the developer can identify nobody from the random ID, say how requests are handled by email.
15. **Legal basis / controller details if the owner is in the EU/UK**: OWNER TO CONFIRM; not drafted here.

**Privacy Policy for Where's the Bottom?**

Effective date: [OWNER TO CONFIRM: date]

Where's the Bottom? ("the game") is made by [OWNER TO CONFIRM: Hot Attic Games, legal name and country]. You can contact us at [OWNER TO CONFIRM: hotatticgames@gmail.com].

**The short version.** The game has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. Your progress stays on your phone. The game checks for game updates over the internet, and you can choose to email us a bug report.

**What stays on your device**

The game saves your rider profile name, snowflakes, upgrades, records, daily challenge and milestone progress, settings, and any run in progress. The game also keeps a short error log of the last few sessions. This information is stored on your device only and is not uploaded by the game. You can delete a profile in the game (Switch profile, then the edit icon, then Delete profile). Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings removes everything stored on the device. [OWNER TO CONFIRM: Android may back up app data to your Google account if backup is turned on for your device.]

**Game updates**

To deliver game updates, the game contacts the update service run by Expo (u.expo.dev) when it starts, from time to time while it is open, and when you tap "Check for updates". This request is sent over HTTPS and includes your IP address (as with any internet request), your device platform, the game's version, update channel and update identifiers, and a random identifier created when the game is installed. If the game suffered a serious crash, a short part of the crash message may be included in the next request. The request does not include your profile name, progress or any other personal information. Expo's handling of this data is described in Expo's privacy policy: [OWNER TO CONFIRM: link].

**Bug reports and feature requests**

If you tap "Send bug report" or "Send feature request" in Settings, the game opens your email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes what you write plus technical details: the game and Android build versions, update details, your device's browser/user-agent text (which usually includes your Android version and phone model), and recent error messages with times. You can read and edit the draft, and nothing is sent unless you press send. We use these emails only to look into the problem and reply to you, and we delete them when we no longer need them or when you ask.
